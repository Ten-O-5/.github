**Empowering Enterprise Modernization and Technology-Driven Growth Through Smart Technology.**

Ten-O-5 is a software development and technology consultancy based in Curaçao. We design and build custom software solutions that help organizations improve processes, automate work, and turn business requirements into reliable digital products.

Our approach combines **software engineering, solution architecture, business process understanding, and practical implementation**.

## What we do

We work with organizations on:

- **Custom Software Development** — Web applications, portals, internal systems, APIs, and integrations.
- **Solution Architecture** — Designing maintainable and scalable technical solutions.
- **Business Process Digitalization** — Turning manual or fragmented processes into effective digital workflows.
- **Systems Integration** — Connecting applications, services, APIs, and third-party platforms.
- **Automation** — Reducing repetitive work and improving operational efficiency.
- **Technical Consulting** — Helping organizations make practical technology and architecture decisions.
- **Implementation & Support** — Taking solutions from concept through production and ongoing operation.

## How we work

We believe good software starts with understanding the problem.

Rather than treating technology as the objective, we look at the business process, the people using it, and the outcome the organization needs. From there, we design and implement technology that fits.

Our projects typically move through:

**Understand → Design → Build → Integrate → Deploy → Improve**

## Technology

Our work spans modern web applications, cloud infrastructure, APIs, integrations, automation, and data-driven systems.

Technology choices are driven by the requirements of each solution rather than by a fixed stack.
